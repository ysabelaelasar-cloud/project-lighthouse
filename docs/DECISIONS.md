# Architectural Decisions

## Decision 001

Recommendations must always be explainable.

Reason:

Users deserve to understand why guidance changes.

---

## Decision 002

Personal history is prioritized over population averages whenever sufficient data exists.

---

## Decision 003

The application informs.

The user decides.

---

## Decision 004

The interface must remain usable on high symptom days.

---

## Decision 005

The primary goal is better decision making—not more data collection.
