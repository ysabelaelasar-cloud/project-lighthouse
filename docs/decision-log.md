Decision #001

Lighthouse recommendations must always be explainable.

Reason:
Users should understand why advice changes from day to day.

Consequence:
Every prediction model must expose its contributing factors.

Decision #002

Recovery scores will prioritize the user's historical patterns over population norms whenever sufficient personal data exists.

Reason:
Chronic illness is highly individualized.

Consequence:
Models require an initial learning period but become increasingly personalized over time.
