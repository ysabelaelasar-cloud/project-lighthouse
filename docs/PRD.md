# Product Requirements Document

## Vision

Project Lighthouse provides personalized decision support for people with fluctuating chronic illnesses.

The goal is to transform fragmented health information into actionable recommendations while remaining transparent and explainable.

---

## Version

Beacon (0.1)

---

## Goals

Beacon should answer four questions:

1. How am I doing today?
2. Why?
3. What has contributed?
4. What should I consider next?

---

## Target User

Initially a single user.

Future versions may support broader populations with chronic illnesses such as:

- hEDS
- POTS
- ME/CFS
- Long COVID
- MCAS

---

## Initial Data Sources

- Visible
- Fitbit
- Google Fit
- Google Calendar
- Medisafe
- Weather
- Manual notes

---

## Success Criteria

Users should understand today's health context within ten seconds of opening the application.
