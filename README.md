# 🏮 Project Lighthouse

> Know your limits before your body has to teach them to you.

## Overview

Project Lighthouse is an explainable AI-powered decision support system designed to help people living with dynamic chronic illnesses make informed decisions about how to spend their limited energy.

Rather than functioning as another symptom tracker or fitness application, Lighthouse combines health data, activity, environmental conditions, medications, calendar events, and historical patterns to estimate daily capacity and provide personalized guidance.

The project began as a personal research initiative and is being built around real-world longitudinal data.

## Long-Term Vision

Help people with fluctuating chronic illnesses understand:

- How they are doing today
- Why they feel that way
- What activities are likely to increase risk
- How to make better pacing decisions

without replacing clinical judgment.
